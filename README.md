## 💫 About Me

I'm a software engineer who loves building backend systems that scale. I work primarily in
**Java** with **Dropwizard**, designing event-driven microservices wired together with
**Kafka** and **SQS**, backed by **Redis**, **DynamoDB**, **MySQL**, and **S3** on AWS.

- ☕ Building production services in **Java** & **Dropwizard**
- 🔗 Architecting **event-driven** systems with **Kafka** & **SQS**
- ☁️ Shipping **cloud-native** applications on **AWS**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I learn best by building things and breaking them (then fixing them)
